# Welcome

Welcome to my research website. I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at The University of Texas at Austin, where I work on understanding chromosome organization and mechanics through polymer-based, data-driven approaches. My broader interests span polymer physics, soft matter, biophysics, and computational modeling.

Here you can find information about my research, publications, and ongoing projects. Feel free to explore.

## Short Bio

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Chemistry at The University of Texas at Austin, working with [Prof. Dave Thirumalai](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=sNiAY9IAAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate). My research lies at the intersection of polymer physics, computational modeling, and biophysics. Currently, I investigate the mechanics and organization of chromosomes using polymer-based, data-driven approaches.

I earned my Ph.D. from [Max Planck Institute for Polymer Research](https://www.mpip-mainz.mpg.de/) in Mainz, Germany, under the supervision of [Prof. Kurt Kremer](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=4EGKVD0AAAAJ). My doctoral work focused on polymer brushes, spanning their growth mechanisms to their applications in nanoparticle organization. During this project, I collaborated closely with [Prof. Regine von Klitzing](https://scholar.google.co.in/citations?user=f9cEXPkAAAAJ&hl=en) at the Technical University of Darmstadt.
