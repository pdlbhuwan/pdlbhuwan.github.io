My research combines polymer physics, computational modeling, and biophysics to understand chromosome organization and mechanics through data-driven approaches.

I earned my Ph.D. from [Max Planck Institute for Polymer Research](https://www.mpip-mainz.mpg.de/) in Mainz, Germany, under the supervision of [Prof. Kurt Kremer](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=4EGKVD0AAAAJ). My doctoral work focused on polymer brushes, spanning their growth mechanisms to their applications in nanoparticle organization. During this project, I collaborated closely with [Prof. Regine von Klitzing](https://scholar.google.co.in/citations?user=f9cEXPkAAAAJ&hl=en) at the Technical University of Darmstadt.
